Dear My Sunflower, ♡

From “Hello, good morning” to “Attracted lang ko sa imoha,” you thought I had something to tell you. But nah, Turns out we got something for each other. Who would've thought we'd come this far?

You thought we would only ever be friends because you said that we were just going to be tropa and nothing more. But I refused to settle for that because I knew I really wanted you, and I knew what I wanted. You know, my life feels more peaceful and filled with love with you in it. Ever since you came into my life, everything has changed. And deep down, I knew I had to meet you halfway, and I don’t regret making that choice. Because, honestly, we’ve both grown together.”

I don't really know where to begin, but I just want you to know that you have become someone special to me. Somehow, even the simplest moments feel a little better whenever you're part of them.

I appreciate your presence, your smile, and the little things that make you who you are. You might not realize it, but you have a way of making ordinary days feel a little more beautiful.

I don't want you to feel pressured by anything I say. We don't have to rush, and we don't need to figure everything out right now. I'm happy to take things one step at a time and let everything happen naturally.

For now, I just want to keep making memories with you, learning more about you, and enjoying whatever this little journey brings us. No expectations, no pressure, just honesty and a little hope.

If loving you is a mistake, then I’m willing to keep making that mistake over and over again. But yeah, truthfully, I will be forever yours, and I always will be. You know, heaven was right when it said, ‘Love is all that I need, and I found it there in your heart.’

Whatever happens, I'm glad our paths crossed. And if this letter can make you smile, even for a moment, then I'm already happy I made it for you. 💗

With love,
Stew ♡
